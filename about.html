Our Story

Founded in the heart of Lucknow, Smile Kraft was born from a singular vision: to redefine the dental experience. We noticed that clinical environments often felt cold and intimidating. We chose a different path.

By combining world-class orthodontic technology with the warmth of luxury hospitality, we created a sanctuary for oral health. Our journey has been defined by the thousands of smiles we've crafted, each a testament to our commitment to precision and patient comfort.
